Install Vanguard: Void Vector directly to your home screen with zero browser bars, fullscreen landscape locking, and offline support:

  • Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge): Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner, then tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
